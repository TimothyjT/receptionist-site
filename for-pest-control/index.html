What happens when a pest control call comes in

  1. A homeowner calls about a scorpion in the living room, a wasp nest over the front door, or ants all over the kitchen. You are on a route and cannot pick up.

  2. Willa answers live, in your company's name. She asks what the pest is and where in the house it is, and checks your real open times.

  3. She books the visit, or takes a clean message. You get a summary of every call, so the booked job and the callback list are waiting when you surface.

  4. If the call is one you said should always reach you — a scorpion inside the house, or stinging insects at the front door with someone allergic in the home, say — it goes straight to you under the rules you set on day one.

Questions pest control owners ask

Does it answer live, or just take a message after?

It answers live, by voice, when you cannot pick up. It answers basic questions from your own services, hours, and prices, books the visit from your real open times, or takes a message with a summary sent to you.

What happens on a call about a scorpion inside, or wasps at the door, that cannot wait?

You set the rules on day one: which calls the voice handles, and which ones go straight to you, every time. A caller with a scorpion inside the house, a wasp nest at the front door and someone allergic in the home, or anything complicated goes where your rules say. The voice does not dispatch your techs.

What does it tell callers about my prices?

Only the prices you give us, loaded the way you say them. If a caller asks for a number it does not have, it does not guess. It takes the question and the caller's number, and you call back with the real number.
