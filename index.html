What does AI Phone Receptionist do?

  1. On one short call, we collect your services, hours, prices, and booking rules — and what should always go straight to you.
  2. We set up the voice on your number. You hear it on a live test call and approve it before it ever answers a real customer.
  3. It answers when you can’t, books the job or takes a clean message — and anything complicated goes straight to a human: you.

A sample moment — shown with made-up names

Sample: The owner is under a house fixing a pipe. The phone rings twice and the receptionist picks up. The caller asks about a leaking water heater; it books Thursday at 9 AM. The owner gets the booking and a short summary — and never stopped working.

Who is AI Phone Receptionist for — and who is it not for?

It’s for you if…

  • You miss calls all day because you’re out on jobs.
  • After-hours callers reach your voicemail and never call back.
  • You run a one- or two-person shop with no one free to answer the phone.

It’s not for you if…

  • You already have a full-time receptionist answering every call.
  • You need emergency dispatch calls handled with no human involved. Real emergencies and hard calls always go to a person.
